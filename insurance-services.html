Force-placed insurance

Cover the gap tracking can't close.

  • Residential property
  • Commercial property
  • Foreclosed property
  • Flood-zone mortgages
  • Consumer loans
  • Automobile loans

Force-placed insurance is how lenders, banks and creditors protect their assets when a renter's or property owner's coverage cancels, lapses, or proves insufficient.

Tracking will tell you the moment a policy cancels or lapses. What it cannot do is force the lessee to renew, or protect you while they don't. A force-placed policy holds that line, keeping your financial interest protected until proper coverage is back in place.

It's available in a wide range of policy types — residential, commercial and foreclosed property, mortgaged property inside a flood zone, and consumer and automobile loans — so your investment stays protected until it's repaid. Rather than carrying the risk of a cancellation or lapse, force-placed coverage protects your bottom line.
