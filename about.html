About us

The most trusted name in insurance tracking.

Since 1987 we have provided the leasing and lending industries with skilled risk management services. We offer highly customized solutions to every customer, so you get the exact lease tracking you need — backed by service we're proud of.

What we do

We focus on what we do best — insurance tracking.

LTS was founded in 1987 to provide risk management services to the leasing and lending industries. At the time that meant both insurance tracking for leased automobiles and contingent/excess liability insurance for leasing companies. Today we concentrate entirely on tracking, which has let us strengthen the offering and stay flexible enough to service all carriers.

We provide tracking for retail and commercial automobile leases, automobile loans, equipment leases and loans, commercial and residential real estate, and floor plan financing.

Why LTS?

Five reasons clients stay with us.

No conflict of interest

We don't offer insurance ourselves, so there's never a conflict of interest in the tracking function. We work with any carrier and conform to their due diligence requirements — including any contingent/excess carrier you use.

Passive and active monitoring

We process every notice received by Electronic Data Interchange (EDI), fax and mail. We also proactively request proof of insurance at renewal — even on policies written as continuous until cancelled — and check carrier websites directly to confirm coverage and renewal.

Certificates scanned and stored

Every insurance certificate we receive is scanned and stored in our secure database, available to you whenever you need it.

Customization that keeps up

We offer numerous customization options to meet your needs now and later — including the wording on our notifications and the timing of when notices go out.

Reporting in the format you need

We can provide special reporting in whatever format you require: PDF documents, website notifications, emails or spreadsheets.
