Insurance tracking

Tracking built around your portfolio.

Lease Tracking Services is the most trusted name in insurance tracking. Since 1987 we have given the leasing and lending industries skilled risk management, with highly customized solutions so you get exactly the tracking you need.

Why choose LTS

A process honed over decades.

Whether you track in-house or outsource, you can benefit from our streamlined process. We've refined it to reduce your administrative burden, cut the losses tied to missing or inadequate coverage, and keep you aware of any issue.

The benefits of our tracking process

  • We work with your customers' carriers and agents to funnel all insurance correspondence to a separate PO Box — cutting the mail your company has to receive and process.
  • Existing agreements with major and minor carriers let us receive electronic notifications daily, so we respond faster than traditional mail allows.
  • Every certificate we receive is scanned into industry-standard PDF and made available to you.
  • Tracking takes hiring, training and executing the process off your plate, with the peace of mind that professionals are handling it.
ActivTrak

How our primary tracking works.
